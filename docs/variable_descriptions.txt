age – Age of the patient (in years).
gender – Biological sex of the patient (male/female).
bmi – Body Mass Index (kg/m²).
surgery – Surgical diagnosis (yes/no).
trauma – Trauma diagnosis (yes/no).
medical – Medical diagnosis (yes/no).
apsiii – Acute Physiology Score III (severity of illness index).
sofa – Sequential Organ Failure Assessment score.
smoker – Smoking status (yes/no).
copd – Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (diagnosis present/absent).
ischemicHd – Ischemic Heart Disease (diagnosis present/absent).
ards – Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (diagnosis present/absent).
death – 90-day mortality outcome (yes/no).
oxygenation – Oxygen therapy treatment arm (conservative vs liberal strategy indicator).
spo2 – Peripheral Oxygen Saturation as measured by pulse oximetry (SpO₂).
fio2 – Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FiO₂).
sao2 – Arterial Oxygen Saturation (SaO₂).
pao2 – Arterial Partial Pressure of Oxygen (PaO₂, mmHg).
paco2 – Arterial Partial Pressure of Carbon Dioxide (PaCO₂, mmHg).
ph – Arterial Blood pH.
lactate – Blood Lactate Level (mmol/L).
hemoglobin – Hemoglobin Concentration (g/dL).
peep – Positive End-Expiratory Pressure (cm H₂O).
vt – Tidal Volume (mL) delivered per breath on the ventilator.
peakAirPressure – Peak Airway Pressure (cm H₂O).
minVentVol – Minute Ventilation Volume (L/min).
